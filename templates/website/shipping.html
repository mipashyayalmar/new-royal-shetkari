Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: {{ biz.policy_updated }}

Food delivery

  • We deliver freshly cooked food only within our local delivery area in {{ biz.city }}. We do not ship food outside the city or by courier.
  • Orders are usually delivered within 30 to 60 minutes of confirmation. At busy times or in bad weather it can take longer; we will tell you if there is a delay.
  • Any delivery charge is shown before you pay. Takeaway orders are ready for pickup at the restaurant at the time we give you.
  • Please make sure the address and phone number are correct and someone is available to receive the order. If we cannot deliver because of a wrong address or no answer, the order may not be refundable once cooked.

Royal Shetkari POS (software)

  • The POS is an online service. Nothing physical is shipped.
  • Your account is activated, and login details sent by WhatsApp or email, within 24 hours of a successful payment (usually much sooner).
  • Printers, tablets and other hardware are not sold by us.
