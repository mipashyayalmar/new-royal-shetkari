Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: {{ biz.policy_updated }}

Food orders

Cancellation

  • You can cancel an order for a full refund before our kitchen starts preparing it. Call or WhatsApp {{ biz.phone }} as soon as possible.
  • Once preparation has started, the order cannot be cancelled, because food is cooked fresh to order.
  • If we cancel your order (for example, an item is unavailable or we cannot deliver to your address), you get a full refund.

Wrong, missing or poor-quality items

  • Report it within 2 hours of delivery or pickup, with a photo where possible.
  • After checking, we will replace the item or refund its price, as you prefer.

Royal Shetkari POS subscription

  • The {{ plan.trial_days }}-day trial is free; nothing is charged if you do not continue.
  • You can cancel the subscription any time. It stays active until the end of the month already paid, and is not renewed.
  • Monthly fees already paid are not refunded for partly used months.
  • If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or charged an incorrect amount, tell us within 7 days and we will refund the extra amount in full.

How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds are made to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet) through our payment partner.
  • We start the refund within 2 working days of approval. It usually reaches your account in 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Cash payments are refunded in cash at the restaurant or by UPI.

Contact

Send refund requests with your order or invoice number to {{ biz.email }} or WhatsApp {{ biz.phone }}.
