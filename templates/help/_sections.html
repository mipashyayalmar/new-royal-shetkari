Your job in this POS

{% if role == "owner" %}

As Owner you can use every restaurant screen: setup, menu, staff, payments, kitchen, billing, stock, customers, reports and closing the day. You also see Guide Access, where you can preview what each staff role sees in this guide. Platform administration (adding restaurants, switching features on or off) is done by the software provider's superuser account, not by the owner.

{% elif role == "manager" %}

As Manager you run the floor and the back office: setup, menu, tables, staff (except owners), payments, discounts, refund requests, stock, purchases, shifts, cash register and reports. Approving refunds is for the owner.

{% elif role == "cashier" %}

As Cashier you take orders, make bills, give discounts, take cash and card payments, and you are one of the people allowed to confirm UPI/QR payments after checking the money arrived. You can also look up customers and bookings.

{% elif role == "captain" %}

As Captain you take orders at tables, send them to the kitchen, make bills, give discounts, take cash and card payments, and look after bookings. A cashier, manager or owner must confirm UPI/QR payments.

{% elif role == "waiter" %}

As Waiter you take orders at tables, send them to the kitchen, serve dishes when they are ready, answer guest calls and make the bill. A cashier, captain, manager or owner takes the payment.

{% elif role == "chef" %}

As Chef you work on the kitchen screen: start dishes, mark them ready and clear finished tickets.

{% elif role == "kitchen" %}

As Kitchen Staff you use the kitchen screen to start dishes and mark them ready.

{% endif %}
